# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome questions asked in good faith, assume mistakes are repairable, and focus reviews on the work rather than the person. Safety concerns about flight behaviour should be raised clearly and without pressure to skip validation.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, or knowingly encouraging unsafe or unlawful operation.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, including public interactions representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [contact@patbaumgartner.com](mailto:contact@patbaumgartner.com). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
